# Virality Loops

Growth and monetization surfaces *beyond* the first onboarding paywall. The onboarding paywall is the
floor, not the ceiling — high-intent moments across the lifecycle justify their own tailored prompts,
and shareable value creates organic acquisition. Keep all of it honest and genuinely useful, never
nagging. Fill the `TAILOR PER APP` blanks.

## Multi-surface monetization

Add a tailored conversion prompt at moments where intent is genuinely high — not on a timer:

- **Feature / usage limit** — user hits a gated capability or runs out of free credits.
- **Post-value moment** — right after the app delivers a clear win.
- **Streak / achievement** — momentum the user won't want to lose.
- **Win-back** — lapsed or declined users, re-engaged gracefully (not guilt-tripped).

## Share / referral loop

The strongest acquisition loop is a **shareable output** the user is proud of (the "value delivery"
moment). Make sharing one tap and make the shared artifact carry the app's value forward.

**`TAILOR PER APP` — what's worth sharing + the referral incentive (if any):** _e.g. "Share the
generated result; both sides get bonus credits."_

## Ethics

Honest means only — no fake scarcity, dark patterns, or manipulative guilt. A loop that burns trust
costs more than it earns.
